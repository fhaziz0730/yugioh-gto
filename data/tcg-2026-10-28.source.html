Forbidden & Limited Lists – October 19, 2026

Effective from October 19, 2026

  • Cards that are "Forbidden" cannot be used in your Main Deck, Extra Deck, or Side Deck.
  • You can only have 1 copy maximum of a "Limited" card in your Main Deck, Extra Deck, and Side Deck combined.
  • You can only have 2 copies maximum of a "Semi-limited" card in your Main Deck, Extra Deck, and Side Deck combined.

The previous (September 21, 2026) list will remain in effect until October 19, 2026.

The change to "Perfectron Hydradrive Dragon" does not take effect until the release of Magnificent Maestros on November 11, 2026.

The next update after this will be in a few months.
